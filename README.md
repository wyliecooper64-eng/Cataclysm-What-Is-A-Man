# Cataclysm: What is a Man?

Cataclysm: What is a Man? is a turn-based survival game set in a post-apocalyptic world. It is a fork of C:DDA, mosty foucsing on lore and fun gameplay instead of realism. This fork keeps the exodii but adds CBMs back to labs. Other changes are has listed:
    
    1. More Factions, such has raiders who block your way unless you pay a toll, or a group who wants tech to their own and hate every other monster.
   
    2. Harder Lab Raids, labs will have stronger zombies and armed ferals who can use guns (they only spawn in labs unlike C:TLG) 
   
    3. Factions sent up camps overtime in the world just like how monster elvoe. Plus more refugee centers and Hub01 locals who talk to eachother.
   
    4. Friendy Mi-Go monsters who fight agaisnt main Mi-Gos. (Don't tel H.P Lovecraft i did that to one of his monsters)
   
    5. MORE LORE
